Inside KANAPET’s Booth at CIPS 2026

It’s the second day of the China International Pet Show in Guangzhou. At Booth G053 in Hall 11.3, a wooden perch rises above one of our bird cages and a clear tunnel loops over a hamster habitat. Both are easy to spot from the aisle.

Published 2026-11-13 · 2 min read

On the white bird cage, two sections of the wire roof lift up to hold the perch between them, giving birds a place to land above the cage. Through the clear front doors, you can see the feeding cups and the perches at different heights inside.

Along the back wall, two arched bird cages sit side by side, one with a pink base and one with a pale blue base. Seeing them together makes it easy to compare the colours for a shop display.

On the hamster display, a clear tunnel runs up the outside of one habitat, over the roof and down the other side. Inside, a yellow wheel stands behind a pink exercise saucer. You can see where the tunnel meets the enclosure and how much floor space the accessories take up.

If you’re choosing cages for your shop or planning a private label range, come and look at the samples with us. We can discuss which colours and accessories suit your range, as well as OEM and ODM projects. You’ll find us in Area B of the China Import and Export Fair Complex through November 15.
