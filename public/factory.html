Our Factory

20,000㎡ vertically integrated manufacturing facility — mold, injection and wire plants all under one roof since 1991.

Since 1991

Integrated Pet Cage Manufacturing

Based in Foshan, Guangdong, Kanapet brings mold making, plastic injection molding and wire forming together to manufacture pet cages for wholesalers and brands. Our in-house teams support product development, production and quality control, from tooling to finished components.
