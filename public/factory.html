Our Factory

20,000㎡ vertically integrated manufacturing facility — mold, injection and wire plants all under one roof since 1991.

Since 1991

Three Decades of Pet Cage Manufacturing

Founded in 1991 in Foshan, Guangdong — the heart of China's manufacturing hub — Kanapet has grown from a small workshop into a 20,000㎡ integrated production base serving wholesalers and brands across North America, Europe and beyond.

What sets us apart is vertical integration: we own and operate three specialized plants — mold making, plastic injection molding, and iron wire forming — eliminating middlemen and giving us full control over quality, cost and lead time.

Our in-house new material laboratory continuously develops high-strength, fall-resistant transparent materials, making us a specialist in the premium transparent cage segment.

  • Founded 1991 — 35+ years manufacturing experience
  • 20,000㎡ factory area in Foshan, Guangdong
  • 3 in-house plants: mold, injection, wire
  • New material R&D laboratory
  • Strict QC from raw material to finished goods
  • Exporting to 30+ countries worldwide
Vertical Integration

Three In-House Plants

Every step of production happens under our roof — no outsourcing, no quality gaps.

🔧

Mold Making Plant

CNC machining centers, EDM and milling machines. We design and build all molds in-house — 30–45 day tooling lead time, with full after-sales mold maintenance.

🏭

Injection Molding Plant

Multiple injection molding machines producing transparent cage panels, bases and accessories. Automated robotic arms for consistent quality and high output.

🔗

Wire Forming Plant

Iron wire cutting, bending, welding and powder coating lines. Producing cage frames in coated iron, 201 and 304 stainless steel.
