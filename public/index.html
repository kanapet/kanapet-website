How We Work

From Your Product Brief to a Ready-to-Sell Product

A practical development path that verifies the product, packaging and production details before your order goes into mass production.

Step 01

Design, 3D Print & Sample Approval

Send a drawing or model, or develop the idea with our team. We confirm the 3D design, make a 3D-printed prototype and refine the product before tooling starts.

Step 02

Tooling, Trial Mold & Packaging Tests

After sample approval, we build injection or wire-forming tooling, then run trial parts for confirmation. Packaging, cartons, instructions, printing and logo details are tested alongside the product.

Step 03

Production, Assembly & Final QC

Once trial parts are approved, production begins. When components and packaging are ready, we assemble and inspect the finished product so it arrives complete and ready for retail.
