Our Factory

Three In-House Plants, Full Control Over Quality

Founded in 1991, Kanapet operates a 20,000㎡ factory in Foshan, China, with three dedicated plants — mold making, injection molding and wire forming — all under one roof.

Our new material laboratory specializes in high-strength, fall-resistant transparent cage materials, giving us a unique advantage in the premium pet cage market.

  • In-house mold design & manufacturing
  • Plastic injection molding plant
  • Iron wire forming & coating plant
  • New material R&D laboratory
  • OEM & ODM full-service capability
  • Strict QC from raw material to finished goods
