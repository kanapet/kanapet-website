43, 62 and 75 transparent cages

Our transparent cage range includes three models: 43 Hamster Transparent Cage, 62 Hamster Transparent Cage, 75 Hamster Transparent Cage. Compare their product dimensions to choose your model.

Procurement questions

What is the minimum order quantity?

MOQ varies by model and configuration. Use each product page as the starting point and confirm the selected colors and carton quantities when requesting a quote.

Can I request my own colors, logo or packaging?

Discuss your private-label requirements with our team. For changes to the product design or tooling, see our OEM & ODM manufacturing services.

What should I include in a quote request?

Include the model, quantity, preferred colors, packaging requirements and destination market. Our team can review the configuration and confirm the order details.

Will any wheel or tunnel fit these cages?

Compatibility depends on the selected cage, connection and accessory dimensions. Send the cage model and accessory list for confirmation before ordering.

How do I confirm packing and delivery requirements?

Review each product page for carton details. Send your destination and order quantity so our team can confirm packing, quotation terms and the current production schedule. No delivery date is implied by the catalog.
