## When and where will CIPS 2026 take place?

The 30th China International Pet Show (CIPS 2026) will take place on **November 12–15, 2026**, at the **China Import and Export Fair Complex, Area B, in Guangzhou, China**. KANAPET will attend the exhibition and welcomes buyers and business partners to meet our team.

| Exhibition detail | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Event | 30th China International Pet Show (CIPS 2026) |
| Dates | November 12–15, 2026 |
| Venue | China Import and Export Fair Complex, Area B |
| City | Guangzhou, China |
| KANAPET hall | Hall 11.3 |
| KANAPET booth | G053 |

## Where is the KANAPET booth?

You can find KANAPET in **Hall 11.3, Booth G053**. Save the hall and booth number before your visit so you can locate our team at the exhibition.

We look forward to meeting wholesalers, distributors, retailers and brands interested in our [bird and small pet products](/products.html). You are welcome to discuss your sourcing plans and product requirements with us in person.

## What are the exhibition opening hours?

The exhibition opens at 10:00 each day. November 12–14 close at 17:30, while the final day, November 15, closes earlier at 14:00. All times below are local Guangzhou time, China Standard Time (UTC+8).

| Date | Opening hours |
| --- | --- |
| Thursday, November 12, 2026 | 10:00–17:30 |
| Friday, November 13, 2026 | 10:00–17:30 |
| Saturday, November 14, 2026 | 10:00–17:30 |
| Sunday, November 15, 2026 | 10:00–14:00 |

Dates, venue and hours were checked against the [official CIPS visitor information](https://en.cipscom.com/CIPS1.htm) on October 9, 2026. Please check the organizer's website for registration instructions and any subsequent schedule updates.

## What can we discuss at the exhibition?

We welcome conversations about wholesale sourcing and [OEM / ODM manufacturing](/oem-odm.html) for bird and small pet products. Share the products you are interested in, your market requirements and any color, label or carton customization needs.

- Existing bird cages, hamster habitats and other pet products in the KANAPET catalog.
- Wholesale sourcing and product selection.
- Custom colors, labels, cartons, assembly and packaging requirements.
- Manufacturing assessment for a custom product supported by 3D drawings or a physical sample.

If you plan to discuss a custom product, prepare your 3D drawings or contact us in advance about a physical sample. Read [our custom product development process](/insights/custom-pet-product-development-process/) for how assessment, quotation, structural optimization, sampling and mold making proceed.

## How can you arrange a meeting with KANAPET?

[Contact KANAPET](/contact.html) before the show with your preferred meeting day and the topics you would like to discuss. This helps us prepare for your visit. You can also email sales@kanapet.com or message us on [WhatsApp](https://wa.me/8615625986407).

We look forward to seeing you in Guangzhou on **November 12–15, 2026 — Hall 11.3, Booth G053**.
