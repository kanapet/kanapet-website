Shanghai, China | August 2026

In August 2026, **Jupiter Joy (宠心悦)**, our domestic pet product brand, exhibited at **Pet Fair Asia 2026 in Shanghai**, showcasing a selection of bird cages, transparent pet enclosures, and practical pet accessories.

Jupiter Joy and **KANAPET** are two brands under the same manufacturing company, serving different markets. Jupiter Joy focuses on China's domestic pet market, while KANAPET specializes in international B2B partnerships, including wholesale supply, OEM manufacturing, and ODM product development.

The exhibition offered an opportunity to present our products in person and demonstrate the design and manufacturing expertise behind our pet product collections.

## Portable Bird Cages Designed for Everyday Convenience

Our portable bird cage collection was one of the featured product categories at the exhibition.

The displayed cages combined metal wire structures with colorful molded plastic bases, integrated carrying handles, and practical accessories, including feeding cups, water bottles, and wooden perches.

Different sizes and configurations were presented to demonstrate options for portability, functionality, and product appearance.

For international pet product distributors and retailers, these designs offer a starting point for building a bird cage collection with different sizes, colors, and accessory configurations.

![Portable bird cage held by its carrying handle, with a green plastic base, metal wire sides, perch and water bottle](/images/insights/jupiter-joy-pet-fair-asia-2026-portable-bird-cage.jpg "Portable bird cage on display at the Jupiter Joy booth.")

## Transparent Pet Enclosures and Multi-Level Cage Designs

Another highlight of our display was our range of transparent pet enclosures and multi-level cage configurations.

Transparent panels provide a clear view of the interior, while modular structures offer different possibilities for enclosure layouts and product configurations.

Our exhibition products demonstrated several areas of our product development experience, including:

- Transparent plastic components and enclosure structures
- Injection-molded bases, frames, and accessories
- Metal wire and plastic component integration
- Multi-level cage configurations
- Functional doors, trays, and removable accessories

These design approaches are relevant to both existing product collections and technically feasible custom development projects.

## Two Brands, One Manufacturing Foundation

Although Jupiter Joy and KANAPET serve different markets, both brands are supported by the same manufacturing foundation.

**Jupiter Joy** focuses on pet products for the Chinese domestic market, including bird cages, small animal housing, and accessories.

**KANAPET** focuses on international business customers seeking pet product manufacturing and wholesale solutions.

Our manufacturing capabilities include plastic injection molding, product development, tooling coordination, component assembly, and quality control.

By combining plastic components with suitable metal and transparent materials, we develop practical pet housing products for different market needs.

Through KANAPET, we work with international importers, wholesalers, distributors, and pet brands.

Supply of existing bird cage, small animal housing, and pet accessory designs, subject to product availability and minimum order requirements.
