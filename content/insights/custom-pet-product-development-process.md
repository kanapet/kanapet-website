## What do you need to provide for a custom pet product assessment?

Provide 3D drawings or send a physical sample to KANAPET. These materials allow us to evaluate the product structure, whether it is reasonable and whether the product can be put into production. An idea alone does not provide enough information for product evaluation or quotation.

- 3D drawings with a physical sample: the preferred starting point for assessment.
- 3D drawings alone: acceptable when a physical sample is not available.
- A physical sample alone: can be assessed, but the initial estimate is less precise without drawings.

For the scope of our manufacturing services, visit our [OEM / ODM page](/oem-odm.html). You can also browse our [existing pet products](/products.html) when considering the type of product you want to develop.

## What should you do if you only have a product idea?

First work with a product designer or design team to turn the idea into 3D drawings. Once the drawings are ready, KANAPET can assess the product's structure and manufacturing feasibility. Preparing this information is necessary to move the custom project forward.

The drawings should represent the product you want us to manufacture. If you also have a physical sample, send it to our company alongside the drawings so we can review both.

## How does KANAPET prepare the initial quotation?

We first evaluate the drawings and any physical sample supplied by the customer, then provide an initial quotation. If the customer accepts this quotation, we proceed to a more detailed analysis of the drawings and product structure. The initial quotation comes before this deeper structural review and optimization.

1. Receive the customer's 3D drawings and, where available, a physical sample.
2. Assess the available information and the product's manufacturing feasibility.
3. Provide an initial quotation for the customer to consider.
4. Proceed to detailed structural analysis after the customer accepts the quotation.

Drawings help improve the accuracy of the assessment. If only a physical sample is available, the estimate will be less precise.

## Why is structural analysis needed before 3D sampling?

After the initial quotation is accepted, we analyze the drawings and product structure in greater detail. Any structural problems need to be revised and optimized before the project advances. The aim is to confirm a structure that can move into production.

A product's appearance alone does not establish that its structure can be manufactured. The drawing review and any necessary changes are part of preparing the product for the next stage.

## When does the project move from 3D sampling to mold making?

After structural analysis and optimization, we proceed with 3D sampling. The sample is reviewed and confirmed before mold making begins. If the sample still has problems, they need to be addressed before committing to production tooling.

1. Complete the detailed structural review and required optimization.
2. Produce the 3D sample.
3. Review and confirm the sample.
4. Begin mold making after sample approval.

For examples of existing product construction, see our [transparent door bird cage](/product/bird-490-double-transparent-door.html). A new custom product still requires its own drawing and sample assessment.

## How long does mold making take, and what happens during mold trials?

Mold making generally takes **15 days to two months**, depending on the size and complexity of the mold. After the mold is made, we carry out a mold trial to produce trial samples. Mass production begins after the mold trial samples are confirmed.

This time range applies to mold making. It does not include the entire project, such as drawing preparation, structural changes, 3D sampling, trial sample confirmation or mass production.

## Which colors, labels and packaging can be customized?

Custom colors, labels and cartons are available. KANAPET assembles and packs the products according to the customer's requirements. Share these requirements as part of the project discussion so the finished product and its packaging can be prepared accordingly.

| Customization | Requirements to provide |
| --- | --- |
| Product colors | The colors required for the product |
| Labels | Label content, artwork and placement requirements |
| Cartons | Carton design and packing requirements |
| Assembly and packaging | How the finished product should be assembled and packed |

![KANAPET branded product packaging](/images/oem/custom-logo-box.webp "Example of KANAPET customized product packaging.")

## How does the project proceed to mass production and shipment?

Once the mold trial samples are confirmed, the project moves into mass production. We assemble and package the products according to the customer's requirements, then ship the finished goods as requested. Custom color, label and carton requirements form part of the agreed finished supply.

The full cooperation process follows this sequence:

1. Customer supplies 3D drawings and, if available, a physical sample.
2. KANAPET evaluates the drawings and samples and provides an initial quotation.
3. Customer accepts the initial quotation.
4. Detailed structural analysis, revisions and optimization take place.
5. 3D sampling is completed and the sample is confirmed.
6. The mold is made, generally within 15 days to two months depending on its size and complexity.
7. Mold trials are carried out and the trial samples are confirmed.
8. Mass production begins.
9. Products are assembled and packaged to customer requirements.
10. Finished goods are shipped according to customer requirements.

To start an assessment, [contact KANAPET](/contact.html) with your 3D drawings or arrange to send a physical sample to our company. Include your requirements for product colors, labels, cartons, assembly and packaging.
